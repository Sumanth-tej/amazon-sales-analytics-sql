# Business Case Study #1

## Company

Amazon

---

## Business Problem

The Sales Manager wants to identify the **Top 5 customers based on their total purchase amount**.

The report should include:

- Customer ID
- Customer Name
- Total Purchase Amount

The results should be sorted in **descending order** based on the total purchase amount.

---

## Business Objective

Identify the highest-value customers by analyzing their total purchase amount. This information helps the business understand customer purchasing behavior and supports customer retention and targeted marketing strategies.

---

## SQL Concepts Used

- INNER JOIN
- Aggregate Function (`SUM`)
- GROUP BY
- ORDER BY
- TOP

---

## Expected Output

A report displaying the **Top 5 customers** with the highest total purchase amount, sorted in descending order.
