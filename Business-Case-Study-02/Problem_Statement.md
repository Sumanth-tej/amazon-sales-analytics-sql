# Business Case Study #2

## Company

Amazon

---

## Business Problem

The Sales Manager wants to identify the **Top 3 products based on their total sales amount**.

The report should include:

- Product ID
- Product Name
- Total Sales Amount

The results should be sorted in **descending order** based on the total sales amount.

---

## Business Objective

Identify the highest-selling products based on their total sales amount. This analysis helps the business optimize inventory management, improve demand forecasting, and support data-driven marketing decisions.

---

## SQL Concepts Used

- INNER JOIN
- Aggregate Function (`SUM`)
- GROUP BY
- ORDER BY
- TOP

---

## Expected Output

A report displaying the **Top 3 products** with the highest total sales amount, sorted in descending order.
