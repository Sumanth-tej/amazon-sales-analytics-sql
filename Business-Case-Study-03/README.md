# Business Case Study #3

## Monthly Sales Analysis

This case study analyzes monthly sales performance using SQL.

### Business Goal

Analyze monthly sales trends by calculating:

- Month
- Total Sales Amount
- Total Number of Orders

The report helps management understand monthly business performance, identify seasonal sales trends, evaluate sales growth, and support future business planning.

### Business Value

This analysis enables the business to:

- Monitor monthly sales performance
- Identify seasonal sales trends
- Support demand forecasting
- Assist management in strategic decision-making

### SQL Concepts Used

- Aggregate Functions (`SUM`, `COUNT`)
- GROUP BY
- ORDER BY
- Date Functions (`DATENAME`, `MONTH`)
