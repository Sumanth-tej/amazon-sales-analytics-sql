# Business Case Study #4

## Company

Amazon

---

## Business Problem

The Regional Sales Manager wants to analyze the sales performance across different regions.

The report should include:

- Region
- Total Sales Amount
- Total Number of Orders

The results should be sorted in **descending order** based on the total sales amount.

---

## Business Objective

Analyze regional sales performance to identify the highest-performing regions. This analysis helps the business evaluate regional demand, optimize resource allocation, improve sales strategies, and support data-driven business decisions.

---

## SQL Concepts Used

- Aggregate Functions (`SUM`, `COUNT`)
- GROUP BY
- ORDER BY

---

## Expected Output

A report displaying each region with its total sales amount and total number of orders, sorted in descending order based on total sales amount.
