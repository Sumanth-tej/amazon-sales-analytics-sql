# Business Case Study #9

## Company

Amazon

---

## Business Problem

The Sales Manager wants to identify **customers whose total sales are higher than the average total sales of all customers**.

The company wants to identify high-value customers who contribute more revenue than the average customer.

The report should display:

* Customer ID
* Customer Name
* Total Sales Amount

### Business Rules

* Calculate the total sales amount for each customer.
* Calculate the average total sales amount across all customers.
* Display **only customers whose total sales are greater than the average customer sales**.
* Sort the report by **Total Sales Amount in descending order**.

---

## Business Objective

Identify customers who generate above-average revenue for Amazon.

This analysis helps the business:

* Identify high-value customers.
* Understand customer revenue contribution.
* Create targeted loyalty programs.
* Prioritize high-value customers for marketing campaigns.
* Support customer retention strategies.
