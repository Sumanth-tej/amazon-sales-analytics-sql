# Business Case Study #7

## Company

Amazon

---

## Business Problem

The Marketing Manager wants to identify **registered customers who have never placed an order**.

These customers have successfully registered on the platform but have not yet placed their first order.

The company plans to target these customers with:

- Welcome offers
- Discount coupons
- Email marketing campaigns
- First purchase incentives

The report should display:

- Customer ID
- Customer Name

### Business Rules

- Display **only customers who have never placed an order**.
- Sort the report by **Customer ID** in ascending order.

---

## Business Objective

Identify inactive customers who have registered but have never placed an order.

This analysis helps the business:

- Increase customer conversion.
- Encourage first-time purchases.
- Improve marketing campaign effectiveness.
- Reduce inactive customer accounts.
- Support customer retention strategies.

---

## SQL Concepts Used

- LEFT JOIN
- NULL Handling (`IS NULL`)
- ORDER BY

---

## Expected Report

A report displaying:

- Customer ID
- Customer Name

The report should include **only customers who have never placed an order** and should be sorted by **Customer ID** in ascending order.
